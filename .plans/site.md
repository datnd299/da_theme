## Brand Positioning

UK Official Store is an activewear and dry-fit clothing store focused on training-ready essentials, comfortable movement, and everyday sportswear style.

Core message:

> Activewear essentials made for movement, comfort, and daily training.

The store should feel:

* Sporty
* Modern
* Clean
* Confident
* Movement-focused
* Practical
* Training-ready
* GMC-safe

The store should not feel like:

* A random general fashion store
* A counterfeit sportswear shop
* An official Nike/Adidas/Puma store
* A luxury streetwear brand
* A body transformation or fitness claim website

## GMC-Safe Rules

The site must present itself as a legitimate activewear store.

Must include:

* Clear product categories
* Contact page
* Shipping policy
* Return & refund policy
* Privacy policy
* Terms of service
* FAQ
* Track order page
* Clear size/material/product details
* No third-party brand affiliation claims

Avoid:

* Counterfeit products
* Fake official claims
* Fake reviews
* Countdown timers
* Unrealistic performance claims
* Body transformation promises
* Medical compression claims
* Copyrighted sports team/brand merchandise

## Shipping Information

Orders are processed within **2–4 business days**.

After dispatch, standard US shipping typically takes **5–10 business days** depending on destination and carrier conditions.

Tracking information is provided once an order ships.

## Return Policy Direction

Customers may request returns within **30 days** of delivery.

Eligible items should be:

* Unworn
* Unwashed
* Undamaged
* In original condition
* With tags and original packaging where applicable

Return copy should be clear, fair, and professional.
