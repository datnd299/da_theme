# SITE.MD — COLLECTIBLE TOY STORE

## 1. Project Overview

Create a modern ecommerce website focused on collectible toys, building sets,
designer figures, art toys, blind-box collectibles, mini figures, display pieces,
and related collector accessories.

The website should feel like a modern collectible culture store rather than a
traditional children's toy shop.

Core concept:

BUILD. COLLECT. DISPLAY.

The experience combines:
- Modern ecommerce
- Collectible culture
- Art and design
- Playful visual elements
- Editorial presentation
- Easy product discovery

The website must remain brand-neutral.
Do not use third-party logos, trademarks, franchise names, copyrighted characters,
or language suggesting official partnerships.

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

Position the store as a curated destination for people who enjoy collecting,
building, displaying, gifting, and discovering creative objects.

Brand personality:
- Modern
- Creative
- Playful
- Curated
- Youthful
- Design-conscious
- Friendly
- Trustworthy

Avoid:
- Childish toy-store aesthetics
- Excessive rainbow colors
- Cheap marketplace appearance
- Overcrowded layouts
- Heavy luxury styling
- Excessive visual effects

The store should sit between:

Modern Ecommerce × Collectible Culture × Design Gallery

---

## 3. Target Audience

Primary audience:
- Teen and adult collectors
- Building-set enthusiasts
- Designer-toy collectors
- Art-toy enthusiasts
- Pop-culture-inspired collectors
- Gift shoppers
- Interior/display collectors

Primary age focus:
16–35+

The interface should remain accessible to parents and casual gift buyers.

---

## 4. Product Ecosystem

Main product categories:

### Building Sets
Creative construction sets, architecture-inspired builds,
vehicles, mechanical builds, decorative builds, and display models.

### Art Figures
Design-focused collectible figures intended primarily for display.

### Designer Toys
Original collectible objects featuring artistic forms,
limited concepts, and creative designs.

### Blind Boxes
Mystery collectible products and series-based mini collectibles.

### Mini Figures
Small collectible characters and display figures.

### Display Collectibles
Larger decorative pieces designed for shelves, desks, and interiors.

### Accessories
Display cases, stands, storage solutions, lighting,
protective products, and collector accessories.

## 30. Brand / Trademark Safety

The website architecture and demo content should remain
independent from third-party brands.

Do not use:

Third-party logos
Trademarked brand names
Franchise names
Recognizable copyrighted characters
Official-looking partnership badges
Unauthorized brand graphics

Use generic demo terminology such as:

Building Set
Designer Figure
Art Figure
Mini Collectible
Blind Box
Display Figure
Collector Edition

Use original placeholder product imagery during development.

Real commercial product information can be integrated separately
when appropriate rights and product data are available.
