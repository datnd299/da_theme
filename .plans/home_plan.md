# Rubyinstar — Home Plan

## Homepage Goal

Create a simple and conversion-focused tire ecommerce homepage for US customers.

Main goals:

* Help customers find the right tires quickly
* Reduce buying confusion
* Show products early
* Build trust before purchase
* Communicate affordability and convenience

Homepage feeling:

* Reliable
* Professional
* Affordable
* Easy shopping experience

Core message:

> Find The Right Tires For Your Vehicle

---

# Homepage Structure

Use 7 sections:

1. Hero + Tire Finder
2. Shop Tires By Category
3. Featured Tire Products
4. Best Deals / Seasonal Picks
5. Why Choose Rubyinstar
6. Customer Feedback Slider
7. Newsletter + Trust Footer

---
