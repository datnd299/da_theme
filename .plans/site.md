# SITE.MD — RELUX WATCHES

## Overview

Relux (Reluxwatches) is an ecommerce store selling **automatic mechanical watches**.

Positioning:

**Mechanical. By Design.**

Target style:

**Minimal × Contemporary × Craftsmanship-Led**

The website should feel premium and mechanical-first without looking traditional, formal, or overly luxurious. Every watch on the site is self-winding automatic — there is no quartz or smartwatch product line.

---

## Audience

Designed primarily for style-conscious customers who want a real mechanical watch without traditional luxury pricing. They value:

* Visible automatic movement (exhibition casebacks)
* Clean, modern design over "old luxury" styling
* A simple, considered shopping experience
* Products that last, backed by a real warranty

## Product Collections

Relux organizes its entire catalog into exactly 3 collections. No other product categories should be introduced.

1. **The Voyager** (`voyager`) — Automatic watches built for the journey. Travel-ready details: GMT-inspired dials, durable stainless steel cases, exhibition casebacks.
2. **The Odyssey** (`odyssey`) — The everyday automatic, refined. Balanced proportions, sunburst dials, versatile finishes for office-to-evening wear.
3. **The Eternal** (`eternal`) — Timeless craftsmanship, made to endure. Slim automatic cases, minimal dials, heirloom-grade finishing.

---

## Warranty

Every Relux watch, across all 3 collections, ships with a **2-year limited warranty** against manufacturing defects in the movement, case, crystal, crown, dial and hands. No registration required. Full terms live on the Warranty Policy page (`/warranty-policy/`) and are referenced from the footer, About page, FAQ, and Terms & Conditions.

---

## Shopping Experience

Prioritize:

**Discover → Compare → Product → Purchase**

Keep navigation simple, product discovery fast, and product pages clean. Every product page and category page should reinforce: automatic movement, materials/finishing, and the 2-year warranty.

Avoid unnecessary categories and complicated navigation — 3 collections only.

---

## Brand Direction

Relux should feel:

**Mechanical
Honest
Modern
Enduring
Accessible Premium**

Avoid:

* Old-fashioned luxury styling
* Excessive black/gold
* Heavy serif typography
* Busy layouts
* Oversized headlines
* Excessive animation
* Quartz/battery messaging — every watch is automatic (self-winding)

---

## Final Rule

**Less decoration. Real movement. Better typography. A warranty you can trust.**
