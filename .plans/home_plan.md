# Bardic — Homepage Plan

# Homepage Objective

The homepage should not feel like a traditional ecommerce storefront.

Its purpose is to:

* emotionally immerse the visitor
* establish craftsmanship and authenticity
* create curiosity
* reduce beginner anxiety
* build premium perception
* guide users toward the hero product

The homepage should feel like:
“A cinematic introduction to handcrafted ancient music.”

---

# Homepage Experience Flow

Emotion
↓
Curiosity
↓
Atmosphere
↓
Trust
↓
Product Desire
↓
Simplicity
↓
Purchase Intent

# Homepage Design Rules

# Contrast Philosophy

Use soft contrast.

Focus on:

* texture
* spacing
* lighting
* typography hierarchy

Not aggressive colors.

---

# Spacing Philosophy

The homepage should breathe.

## Major Sections

128–160px spacing.

## Internal Padding

64–96px.

---

# Motion Philosophy

Motion should feel:

* cinematic
* slow
* atmospheric

Avoid:

* flashy transitions
* excessive movement

---

# Conversion Philosophy

The homepage should sell:

* imagination
* craftsmanship
* atmosphere
* identity
* creative ritual

before:

* specifications
* technical details
* ecommerce mechanics
