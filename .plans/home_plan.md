# MegaMallDepot — Homepage Plan


## Overview


MegaMallDepot is a premium Malaysian kitchen & home essentials retailer. The homepage should inspire everyday cooking and organized living through warm lifestyle imagery while making shopping simple and enjoyable.


---


## Homepage Goals


* Inspire customers through beautiful kitchen lifestyle photography
* Highlight practical kitchen essentials
* Build trust with Malaysian shoppers
* Make product discovery effortless
* Guide visitors naturally from inspiration to purchase


---

# Product Card Rules


Every card should include:


* Large product image
* Product name
* Price
* Rating
* Quick View
* Add to Cart


Use subtle hover animations only.


---


# Content Guidelines


Content should be:


* Friendly
* Helpful
* Warm
* Practical
* Lifestyle-focused
* Original


Avoid:


* Hard-selling language
* Fake urgency
* Marketplace appearance
* Keyword stuffing


---


# Image Direction


Use:


* Bright modern kitchens
* Wooden countertops
* Premium cookware
* Natural lighting
* Malaysian family lifestyle
* Organized kitchen spaces


Avoid:


* White cutout images only
* Dark themes
* Busy promotional graphics
* Warehouse-style photography


---


# Mobile Experience


* Responsive editorial hero
* Two-column product grids
* Large touch-friendly buttons
* Sticky navigation
* Fast loading experience


---


# Ecommerce Direction


Homepage should demonstrate:


* Clear kitchen & home niche
* Original lifestyle content
* Transparent shopping policies
* Strong brand identity
* Mobile-first experience
* Trustworthy shopping journey
