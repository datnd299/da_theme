# Proudlywear E-commerce Design Skill

## 1. Website Identity

**Website:** Proudlywear.com
**Market:** United States
**Business Type:** Patriotic POD apparel and custom gift store
**Core Products:** Veteran polos, patriotic shirts, hats, mugs, accessories, America 250 products, and personalized military-inspired gifts.

**Positioning:** Patriotic Apparel & Custom Gifts For Veterans, Military Families, And Proud Americans.

**Core Message:** Honor The Service. Wear The Legacy.

**Brand Feeling:** Patriotic, respectful, emotional, trustworthy, American, veteran-inspired, gift-focused, premium POD, easy to shop.

**Avoid:** Cheap POD marketplace, political campaign site, fake official military store, generic apparel website, aggressive military visuals, and low-trust dropshipping style.

---

## 2. Core Audience

Primary customers are U.S. veterans, military families, patriotic Americans, fathers, husbands, grandfathers, and gift buyers.

Main shopping occasions include Veterans Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Father’s Day, Christmas, and America 250th Anniversary.

Customers buy because they want to honor service, remember family legacy, wear American pride, or give a meaningful personalized gift.

---

## 3. Compliance Rules

Never claim official military, government, VA, or licensed status unless legally verified.

Avoid phrases like: official U.S. Army, official Marine Corps, official Navy, official Air Force, official government product, VA approved, licensed military merchandise, government approved, official veteran uniform.

Do not use official military seals, unauthorized military logos, government badges, fake veteran endorsements, fake reviews, fake charity donation claims, or fake customer stories.

Use safe wording: veteran-inspired, military tribute, service-inspired, patriotic apparel, custom veteran gift, for veterans and military families, American pride apparel.

---

## 4. Approved Brand Language

Use often: patriotic apparel, veteran-inspired apparel, custom veteran gifts, personalized veteran apparel, military tribute gifts, American pride, service legacy, honor service, carry the legacy, military family gifts, America 250th Anniversary, Father’s Day gifts for veterans, Veterans Day gifts, custom name, rank, service years.

Tone should be respectful, proud, simple, emotional, older-audience friendly, and gift-focused.

Avoid political anger, partisan messaging, fake urgency, aggressive military wording, and overly dramatic sacrifice copy.

---

## 5. Color System

Use a mature patriotic palette, not a cheap flag collage.

**Deep Navy:** `#0B1F3A` — header, footer, hero, dark trust sections.
**Heritage Red:** `#B31942` — CTA, prices, badges, hover states.
**Antique White:** `#F7F2E8` — warm heritage backgrounds.
**Clean White:** `#FFFFFF` — product cards, forms, policy pages.
**Heritage Gold:** `#C6A15B` — premium accents, gift highlights, America 250 details.
**Charcoal Ink:** `#111827` — headings and main text.
**Muted Gray:** `#6B7280` — body text, helper text, policy notes.
**Border Gray:** `#E5E7EB` — borders, cards, form fields, dividers.

Red is for conversion. Gold is for premium tribute accents. Deep Navy should define the brand foundation.

---

## 6. Typography

Recommended fonts: Inter, Manrope, Plus Jakarta Sans, Barlow Condensed, Oswald, Libre Franklin.

Best pairing: Headings use Barlow Condensed or Oswald. Body and UI use Inter or Manrope.

Avoid Comic Sans, Times New Roman, script fonts, thin luxury serif UI, and heavy military stencil fonts.

Type scale: Hero heading `text-4xl md:text-6xl lg:text-7xl font-bold tracking-tight leading-none`. Section heading `text-3xl md:text-4xl lg:text-5xl font-bold tracking-tight leading-tight`. Product name `text-sm md:text-base font-semibold`. Price `text-base md:text-lg font-bold text-[#B31942]`. Body text `text-sm md:text-base leading-7 text-[#6B7280]`. CTA text `text-sm uppercase tracking-[0.06em] font-extrabold`.

---

## 7. Layout Rules

Use a clean, product-heavy e-commerce layout.

Container: `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 md:px-6`.
Section padding: `py-12 md:py-16 lg:py-20`.
Product grid: 2 columns mobile, 3 tablet, 4 desktop.
Category grid: 1 column mobile, 2 tablet, 4 desktop.
Product-heavy sections should show 8 products when possible.
Card radius: `rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`.

Prices, CTAs, and product images must always be visible. Do not hide prices on hover.

Avoid overly soft feminine shapes, oversized empty spacing, hidden product grids, and old Bootstrap-style layouts.

---

## 8. Homepage Structure

Use 6–7 sections maximum.

1. Hero + Featured Best Sellers Strip
2. Shop By Collection
3. Best Sellers Product Grid
4. Personalized Veteran Apparel Feature
5. Gift By Occasion / America 250 Collection
6. Customer Tribute / Feedback Slider
7. Trust + About + Newsletter CTA

Homepage must be product-heavy, emotionally clear, trust-focused, short enough to scroll, and easy to shop.

---

## 9. Header Rules

Desktop header: `[Logo] [Best Sellers | Veteran Polos | Veteran Hats | America 250 | Custom Gifts | Accessories] [Search] [Track Order] [Cart]`.

Mobile header: `[Menu] [Centered Logo] [Search] [Cart]`.

Required elements: visible search, cart count, track order link, clear category navigation.

Announcement bar examples: 30% OFF Limited Time, Free Shipping On Orders Over $49, Personalized Veteran Gifts Available.

Do not use fake urgency or sticky banners that block content.

---

## 10. Hero Rules

Hero must include headline, product-focused subheadline, primary CTA, secondary CTA, product/lifestyle image, and a small trust line.

Approved headlines: Honor The Service. Wear The Legacy. / Personalized Veteran Apparel Made With Pride. / Wear Your Name. Carry Your Service. Share Your Pride. / Patriotic Apparel Made To Honor Service And American Pride.

Approved subheadline: Shop custom veteran polos, patriotic hats, mugs, accessories, and America-inspired gifts made for veterans, military families, and proud Americans.

Approved CTAs: Shop Best Sellers, Customize Yours, Shop Veteran Polos, Explore America 250 Collection, Find A Gift For Dad.

Hero image direction: custom veteran polo, patriotic hat, American flag background, folded gift set, older veteran lifestyle image, or product close-up showing name/rank/service years.

Avoid official logos, fake military endorsement, political visuals, AI-looking faces, and too much text inside images.

---

## 11. Product Card Rules

Each product card must show product image, product title, price, sale price if available, and visible CTA.

Preferred CTAs: View Product, Customize Now, Shop Now.

Image ratio: Apparel uses 3/4 or 4/5. Hats, mugs, and accessories use 1/1. Mixed grids should use 1/1 for consistency.

If customizable, show a small badge: Customizable, Personalized, Add Name / Rank.

Do not use fake star ratings, fake “sold today” counters, or fake scarcity.

---

## 12. Collection System

Main collections: Best Sellers, Veteran Polo Shirts, Veteran Hats, America 250 Collection, Custom Military Gifts, Patriotic Accessories.

Optional future collections: Army Veteran Gifts, Navy Veteran Gifts, Air Force Veteran Gifts, Marine Veteran Gifts, Father’s Day Gifts, Veterans Day Gifts, Memorial Day Gifts, Independence Day Gifts, Christmas Gifts For Veterans.

Only add optional collections if there are enough products.

Collection cards should include image, title, short emotional copy, and CTA.

---

## 13. Personalized Product Rules

Personalized products must clearly show custom name, rank if applicable, service years if applicable, branch/design option if applicable, size, color, production time note, and return note.

Required note: Please review all personalization details carefully before placing your order. Personalized items may require additional production time and may not be eligible for return unless defective, damaged, incorrect, or required by law.

Customization instructions must be visible, not hidden inside a long description.

---

## 14. Best Sellers Grid

Best Sellers should appear early.

Recommended WooCommerce query:

```php
wc_get_products(array(
  'status'  => 'publish',
  'limit'   => 8,
  'orderby' => 'popularity',
  'order'   => 'DESC',
));
```

Fallback query:

```php
wc_get_products(array(
  'status'  => 'publish',
  'limit'   => 8,
  'orderby' => 'date',
  'order'   => 'DESC',
));
```

Use 2 columns mobile and 4 columns desktop.

---

## 15. Gift By Occasion

Use occasion cards such as Father’s Day Gifts, Veterans Day Gifts, Memorial Day Gifts, Independence Day Gifts, America 250th Anniversary, and Christmas Gifts For Veterans.

Copy examples: A meaningful gift for the veteran who carries the story. / Personalized apparel made to honor service years, family legacy, and American pride. / Patriotic gifts for fathers, grandfathers, husbands, and military families.

Avoid guilt-based or overly dramatic copy.

---

## 16. Customer Tribute / Feedback Slider

Use this section to build trust and emotional value.

Do not use fake reviews, fake names, fake photos, or fake star ratings.

Safe title: Customer Tributes & Gift Moments.

Safe intro: Many customers choose personalized veteran apparel as a way to honor service, remember family legacy, and give a gift with meaning.

Safe slide examples: A gift that helps families honor a father’s years of service. / A personalized polo that carries name, rank, and service years with pride. / A patriotic keepsake for Veterans Day, Father’s Day, and everyday wear. / A simple way to show American pride without saying too much.

Do not present these as verified reviews unless they are real.

---

## 17. Trust Signals

Required trust cards: Secure Checkout, Tracking Included, 30-Day Returns, Personalization Support.

Secure Checkout: A safe and simple checkout experience for every order.
Tracking Included: Tracking details are provided once your order ships.
30-Day Returns: Eligible non-personalized items may be returned within 30 days of delivery.
Personalization Support: Review custom name, rank, and service details carefully before ordering.

---

## 18. Footer Requirements

Footer must include About Us, Contact Us, Shipping Policy, Return & Refund Policy, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, FAQ, and Track Order.

Footer should include support email, business hours, short brand description, and custom product note.

---

## 19. Performance & Mobile

Use optimized images, `loading="eager"` for hero images, `loading="lazy"` below the fold, `font-display: swap`, and defer non-critical JavaScript.

Mobile requirements: 2-column product grid, CTA height at least 44px, visible cart, easy search, readable product titles, visible prices, products appear early, and no blocked content.

---

## 20. Accessibility

Required: alt text for all images, contrast at least 4.5:1, clear focus states, ARIA labels for icons, keyboard-friendly slider controls, and readable font sizes.

Alt text examples: Custom veteran polo shirt with name and service years. / Patriotic embroidered-style hat for veterans. / America 250th anniversary apparel gift.

---

## 21. Pre-Output Checklist

Before outputting HTML/Tailwind, verify: hero has clear CTA, products appear early, prices are visible, grid uses 2 columns mobile and 4 desktop, best sellers show 8 products if available, CTA buttons are high contrast, custom product notes are clear, no official military claims, no fake reviews, no fake charity claims, no unauthorized logos, trust signals are present, footer has policy links, and mobile UX is clean.

---

## Final Principle

A Proudlywear page succeeds when customers feel: this product honors someone’s service, this gift feels meaningful, this website looks trustworthy, I understand what I am buying, and I can customize and checkout confidently.

Design must be patriotic, emotional, respectful, product-heavy, and conversion-first.
