# UK Official Store — Design System

## Design Goal

Build a clean, sporty activewear website for dry-fit t-shirts, tracksuits, tank tops, training sets, and gym wear.

The site should feel:

* Modern
* Sporty
* Clean
* Confident
* Movement-focused
* Trustworthy
* GMC-safe

Avoid any design that feels like a fake branded sportswear store, random fashion shop, or overly aggressive gym/bodybuilding website.

---

## Color Palette

### Primary Navy

```txt
#0B1F33
```

Use for headings, footer, strong sections, main text accents.

### Electric Blue

```txt
#2563EB
```

Use for buttons, links, active states, sporty highlights.

### Cool Gray

```txt
#F3F5F7
```

Use for section backgrounds and product grid areas.

### White

```txt
#FFFFFF
```

Use for main background, cards, product sections.

### Charcoal

```txt
#1F2937
```

Use for body text and product titles.

### Lime Accent

```txt
#A3E635
```

Use lightly for sporty badges or small highlight details only.

---

## Typography

Use clean modern sans-serif fonts:

```txt
Inter
Manrope
Plus Jakarta Sans
Source Sans 3
```

Rules:

* Headings: bold, short, clear
* Body text: readable, simple, not too long
* Product titles: clean and direct
* Avoid decorative or luxury serif fonts

---

## Layout Style

Use a clean ecommerce layout:

* Large hero section
* Clear category cards
* Product-focused grids
* Strong visual hierarchy
* Plenty of whitespace
* Short copy blocks
* Mobile-first spacing

Recommended section width:

```txt
max-w-7xl
```

Recommended spacing:

```txt
Desktop: py-20
Mobile: py-14
```

---

## Homepage Sections

Use 5 sections:

1. Hero — Activewear Essentials For Everyday Movement
2. Shop By Category
3. Dry-Fit Training Tops
4. Tracksuits & Gym Wear
5. Customer Care / Trust

Keep homepage short, focused, and category-clear.

## Image Direction

Use realistic activewear images:

* Gym training
* Outdoor workout
* Running track
* Urban fitness lifestyle
* Studio product shots
* Tracksuit lifestyle
* Dry-fit shirt flat lay

Image style:

* Clean
* Sporty
* Realistic
* Sharp fabric texture
* No fake major brand logos
* No heavy sale text inside images
* No overly sexual gym poses

---

## Product Card Style

Product cards should be simple:

* White background
* Light border
* Rounded corners
* Clean product image
* Product title
* Price
* Simple CTA

Avoid:

* Too many sale badges
* Fake reviews
* Countdown timers
* Keyword-stuffed product names

---

## Buttons

### Primary Button

```txt
Background: #2563EB

Hover: #0B1F33
```

### Secondary Button

```txt
Background: transparent / white
Border: #2563EB
Text: #2563EB
Hover background: #F3F5F7
```

Button style:

* Rounded 10–14px
* Medium-bold text
* Clean, sporty, not flashy

---

## Trust Elements

Always include:

* Secure checkout
* Tracking included
* 30-day returns
* Support email
* Shipping timeline
* Clear size/material details

Trust section can use navy background with white text.

---

## GMC-Safe Rules

Do not use:

* Nike / Adidas / Puma style claims
* “Official” affiliation with another brand
* Counterfeit logos
* Medical compression claims
* Fat-loss/body transformation claims
* Fake reviews
* Aggressive countdown sale design

Safe wording:

```txt
movement
comfort
training-ready
lightweight feel
dry-fit style
everyday activewear
sportswear essentials
```

---

## Final Design Feeling

UK Official Store should look like:

> A clean, modern activewear store for dry-fit t-shirts, tracksuits, tank tops, and gym wear made for comfort, movement, and daily training.

The site must feel independent, trustworthy, activewear-focused, and GMC-safe.
