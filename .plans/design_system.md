# MegaMallDepot — Design System


## Design Direction


Inspired by premium kitchen and home retailers with a clean, warm and lifestyle-focused shopping experience.


Website should feel:


* Warm
* Modern
* Elegant
* Organized
* Minimal
* Kitchen-first


Customers should feel they are browsing a premium kitchen lifestyle store that inspires cooking and better home living.


Avoid:


* Marketplace appearance
* Electronics-first layouts
* Flash sale styling
* Overstock product grids
* Busy promotional graphics

# Layout System


Container


```txt
max-w-7xl mx-auto
```


Spacing


```txt
py-16 md:py-24
```


Grid


```txt
2–4 columns
```


Keep generous whitespace throughout the page.


---


# Header


Structure


```txt
Announcement Bar


Logo


Search


Account


Wishlist


Cart


Category Navigation
```


Header should remain simple, bright and easy to navigate.


---


# Hero Section


Use premium kitchen lifestyle photography.


Layout:


50% imagery


50% content


Content:


* Small tagline
* Elegant heading
* Short introduction
* Primary CTA


Avoid cluttered hero banners.


---


# Homepage Components


Use:


* Lifestyle Hero
* Shop By Category
* Featured Collections
* Best Sellers
* Editorial Kitchen Story
* Customer Reviews
* Newsletter


Homepage should alternate between inspiration and shopping.


---


# Product Cards


Display:


* Large product image
* Product name
* Price
* Rating
* Quick View


Hover:


* Soft zoom
* Gentle shadow


Avoid oversized discount labels.


---


# Image Direction


Use:


* Modern kitchens
* Home cooking
* Wooden cutting boards
* Premium knives
* Kitchen organization
* Dining lifestyle
* Bright natural lighting


Products should appear naturally inside real kitchens.


Avoid:


* White cutout images only
* Dark environments
* Commercial warehouse feeling
* Busy graphics

# Motion


Use:


* Fade In
* Soft hover
* Gentle image zoom

# Visual Style


Each section should alternate between:


* Lifestyle storytelling
* Kitchen inspiration
* Product collections


The homepage should resemble a premium kitchen lifestyle magazine while maintaining a smooth ecommerce shopping experience.
