# luxurytheme.com — SITE.md

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** luxurytheme.com
**Project Type:** Luxury Watch E-commerce / Brand Website
**Temporary Brand Name:** luxurytheme.com
**Primary Industry:** Luxury Watches, Fine Timepieces, Premium Accessories
**Website Positioning:** Modern luxury, refined craftsmanship, premium digital shopping experience

luxurytheme.com is a premium luxury watch website designed to present high-end timepieces through a sophisticated, modern, and highly polished digital experience.

The website should combine the visual elegance of luxury fashion brands with the usability and clarity of a modern e-commerce platform.

The overall experience should feel:

* Premium
* Refined
* Minimal
* Modern
* Editorial
* Trustworthy
* Exclusive
* Easy to navigate
* Visually immersive
* Product-focused

The website should never feel overcrowded, overly decorative, outdated, or difficult to use.

Luxury should come from strong typography, spacing, photography, motion, materials, proportions, and attention to detail rather than excessive ornamentation.

---

# 2. Core Brand Direction

## Brand Personality

luxurytheme.com should communicate the personality of a modern luxury watch house.

The brand personality should feel:

**Sophisticated**
Every visual and interaction should feel carefully considered.

**Timeless**
The design should avoid short-lived visual trends that may quickly feel outdated.

**Modern**
Layouts, navigation, transitions, product discovery, and shopping interactions should feel contemporary.

**Confident**
The interface should use strong composition and restraint rather than excessive explanation.

**Exclusive**
Products should feel desirable and carefully curated.

**Approachable**
Although the brand is premium, the interface should remain simple and friendly.

**Craftsmanship-focused**
Product details, materials, movements, manufacturing, and heritage should be presented with importance.

---

# 3. Brand Positioning

luxurytheme.com should position itself between:

* Traditional luxury watch boutiques
* Modern luxury fashion websites
* Premium editorial magazines
* High-end digital commerce platforms

The website should provide the emotional experience of entering a luxury boutique while maintaining the convenience of a modern digital store.

The experience should emphasize:

**Desire → Discovery → Education → Confidence → Purchase**

Users should first become emotionally interested in the watches, then explore collections, understand the product, trust the brand, and finally feel comfortable making a purchase or inquiry.

# 27. Motion Philosophy

Animations should be subtle.

Recommended interactions:

* Image reveal
* Fade transitions
* Slow zoom
* Product hover
* Navigation transitions
* Scroll reveal
* Gallery transitions
* Text mask reveal

Motion duration should generally feel slower and smoother than standard consumer websites.

Avoid:

* Bouncy animation
* Excessive parallax
* Constant movement
* Fast aggressive transitions

---

# 28. Responsive Philosophy

The experience must be designed mobile-first while preserving the luxury character.

Important mobile considerations:

* Large tap targets
* Simple navigation
* Swipe galleries
* Sticky purchase controls
* Readable typography
* Minimal interface clutter

The mobile version should not simply be a compressed desktop layout.

---

# 29. Accessibility

Premium design must remain accessible.

Target:

**WCAG 2.2 AA where practical.**

Important requirements:

* Strong text contrast
* Keyboard navigation
* Visible focus states
* Accessible forms
* Semantic HTML
* Meaningful alternative text
* Screen-reader labels
* Reduced-motion support

Accessibility should be incorporated into the design system rather than added later.

---

# 30. Performance

Core Web Vitals should remain a priority.

# 39. Site Quality Standard

Every page should pass four checks.

## Visual Quality

Does the page feel premium and refined?

## UX Quality

Can users understand and navigate the page easily?

## Product Quality

Does the watch remain the primary focus?

## Technical Quality

Is the page fast, responsive, accessible, and maintainable?

A page should not be considered finished until all four conditions are satisfied.
