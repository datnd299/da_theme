## 2. Brand Positioning

### Positioning Statement

> **America's graphic apparel store** — premium T-shirts, hoodies, bomber hats, and patriotic accessories built for veterans, proud Americans, and anyone who believes freedom deserves to be worn.

### Core Message

> **Wear The Freedom. Live The Pride.**

### Alternative Messages

| *For those who served, those who remember, and those who believe.* | Veteran collections |

| *Your service. Your style. Your statement.* | Personalized products |

### Brand Personality

| Trait | Expression |
|---|---|
| **Patriotic** | Red, white & blue palette; flag motifs; eagle iconography |
| **Respectful** | Honest tribute language; no fake military endorsements |
| **Rugged** | Bomber jackets, distressed graphics, thick fabric feel |
| **Authentic** | Hand-drawn–style graphics; no cheap AI generics |
| **Inclusive** | Veteran *and* civilian; men, women, kids |

### What The Brand IS

- A premium graphic apparel & patriotic gear store
- A veteran-respecting, service-honoring brand
- An easy-to-shop POD store with broad size/color range
- A gifting destination for military families
- A lifestyle brand for American pride

### What The Brand IS NOT

- ❌ An official U.S. military store
- ❌ A political campaign merchandise site
- ❌ A cheap low-quality POD marketplace
- ❌ A tactical / survival gear retailer
- ❌ A fast-fashion trend store

### Lexicon — Always Use

```
patriotic apparel
veteran-inspired gear
American-made spirit
freedom wear
tribute collection
military family gifts
custom veteran gear
service & sacrifice
worn with pride

graphic tees for patriots

best flag t-shirts
USA pride
home of the brave
land of the free
```

### Lexicon — Use With Care

```
army veteran  — only for actual veteran-focused products
navy tribute  — when specifically Navy-inspired
air force proud — ditto
marine corps spirit — ditto
coast guard tribute — ditto
service member — generic, safe
branch of service — safe descriptor
```

### Lexicon — NEVER Use (Unless Legally Verified)

```
official U.S. Army / Navy / Air Force / Marines / Coast Guard merchandise
licensed military gear
VA-approved
government-approved
certified veteran product
official military uniform replica
endorsed by the Department of Defense
```

### Tone of Voice

| Quality | Example |
|---|---|
| **Respectful** | "Worn by those who served — loved by those who remember." |
| **Proud** | "This is freedom you can wear." |
| **Simple** | "Premium graphic tees. American pride. No politics." |
| **Emotional (gift context)** | "A gift that says thank you better than words." |
| **Rugged** | "Built tough. Made to last. Worn with honor." |
| **Clear** | "Customize with your name, branch, and service years at checkout." |

### DO

- Keep headlines under 10 words
- Use short paragraphs (max 3–4 sentences)
- Put price and CTA above the fold
- Address the buyer directly ("You", "Your")
- Use emojis sparingly but intentionally (flag, medal, jacket)

### DON'T

- Use political slogans, candidate names, or party symbols
- Fake urgency ("Only 3 left!") unless real
- Exaggerate military affiliation
- Use aggressive CAPS-LOCK copy in body text
- Mention "official" anything without proof

## 6. Compliance & Legal

### Mandatory Disclaimers

- "This product is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any branch of the U.S. military or government."
- "Veteran-inspired designs are tributes to service. We respectfully honor all who serve and have served."
- "Personalized items: please review all customization details before ordering. May not be eligible for return unless defective."

### Prohibited Elements

- ✅ American flag (generic, no specific military unit)
- ✅ Eagle, liberty bell, stars & stripes patterns
- ✅ "Veteran" as a descriptor
- ✅ Generic branch colors (OD green, Navy blue, Air Force blue, Marine red)
- ❌ Official unit patches or logos
- ❌ Medal of Honor / Purple Heart / specific award imagery (without license)
- ❌ Government seals or DoD marks
- ❌ Political candidate names or campaign slogans

### Design Compliance Quick Reference

| Element | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Flag | Waving flag, vintage flag, distressed flag | Flag draped as superhero cape; flag with political text |
| Eagle | American bald eagle (generic) | Eagle with official government shield |
| Veteran text | "Veteran," "I Served," "Proud Vet" | Specific unit names, deployment locations |
| Branch colors | Use as background accents | Use as claim of official affiliation |

## 8. Visual Direction

### Mood & Feeling

- **Warm Americana** — not cold, not sterile
- **Rugged Heritage** — thick fabrics, worn-in graphics, vintage filters
- **Respectful Tribute** — never cartoonish or mocking
- **Premium POD** — clean mockups, accurate colors, realistic fits

### Photography Style

| Type | Recommended | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Product shots** | Flat lay on wooden table, model wearing on brick wall / street | White-only studio (too sterile) |
| **Lifestyle** | Veteran (or model) in everyday setting, flag visible but not forced | Stock photos of soldiers in combat |
| **Group shots** | Family gifting moment, father & son, couple | Crowds, rallies, protests |
| **Details** | Close-up of embroidery, flag texture, fabric weave | Blurry / compressed images |

### Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Patriot Navy** | `#0B1F3A` | Header, footer, hero backgrounds, dark trust sections |
| **Heritage Red** | `#B31942` | CTAs, prices, badges, sale tags, accent lines |
| **Antique White** | `#F7F2E8` | Warm section backgrounds, tribute sections |
| **Clean White** | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, product backgrounds, forms |
| **Heritage Gold** | `#C6A15B` | Premium accents, America 250, limited-edition badges |
| **Flag Blue** | `#1E3A8A` | Secondary accents, link hover states |
| **Charcoal Ink** | `#111827` | Main headings and body text |
| **Muted Gray** | `#6B7280` | Secondary text, captions, helper notes |
| **Border Gray** | `#E5E7EB` | Card borders, dividers, form outlines |

### Typography

| Role | Font | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hero / Display** | Barlow Condensed | 700, 800, 900 | Bold, tight letter-spacing, all-caps optional |
| **Section Headings** | Barlow Condensed | 600, 700 | Compact, scannable |
| **Product Names** | Inter | 600 | Readable at small sizes |
| **Body / UI** | Inter | 400, 500, 600 | Clean, accessible |
| **Price** | Inter | 700, 800 | Heritage Red color |
| **Badges / Labels** | Barlow Condensed | 700 | Uppercase, small |

> Fallback: `system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`

### Iconography

- Use solid, bold icons (Font Awesome Solid or Heroicons Solid)
- Icon badges on category cards: flag, jacket, hat, tee, medal, gift
- Trust section icons: shield, truck, calendar, headset
- Avoid thin, luxury-style line icons

## 10. Trust Architecture

### Required Trust Elements (Visible Site-wide)

- ✅ Secure Checkout (SSL + badges)
- ✅ Tracking included on all orders
- ✅ 30-day returns (non-personalized items)
- ✅ Personalization support & review reminder
- ✅ Contact page with email & hours
- ✅ Shipping policy (clear, transparent)
- ✅ Return policy (clear, transparent)
- ✅ Privacy Policy
- ✅ Terms of Service
- ✅ Track Order page
- ✅ FAQ page

### Trust Messaging Do's & Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Secure checkout guaranteed" | "100% safe" (overpromise) |
| "Tracking provided on all orders" | "Free 2-day shipping" (unless true) |
| "30-day returns on eligible items" | "No questions asked returns" (legal risk) |
| "Review your custom details before ordering" | "We check everything for you" (liability) |

## 11. Shipping & Returns Framework

### Shipping Parameters

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Order Cutoff | 5:00 PM PST |
| Handling Time | 1–2 business days (Mon–Fri) |
| Transit Time | 5–7 business days (Mon–Fri) |
| Estimated Delivery | Usually 6–9 business days |
| Custom/Personalized Note | May require additional production time before shipment |
| Tracking | Provided after carrier handoff |

### Return Parameters

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Return Window | 30 days from delivery |
| Restocking Fee | $0 for eligible returns |
| Condition | Unused, unworn, unwashed, undamaged, original packaging |
| Defective / Incorrect / Damaged | Store covers return shipping after approval |
| Customer Remorse | Customer pays actual return shipping |
| Refund Timing | Up to 7 business days after inspection & approval |
| Personalized Items | Not eligible unless defective, damaged, incorrect, or required by law |

### Custom Product Note (Mandatory)

> *Please review all personalization details carefully before placing your order. Personalized items may require additional production time and may not be eligible for return unless defective, damaged, incorrect, or required by law.*
