# SITE.MD — RELUX WATCHES

## Overview

Relux Watches is a modern ecommerce store for watches and watch accessories.

Positioning:

**Modern Watches for Modern Life.**

Target style:

**Minimal × Contemporary × Youthful × Refined**

The website should feel premium without looking traditional, formal, or overly luxurious.

---

## Audience

Designed primarily for modern, style-conscious customers who value:

* Clean design
* Everyday watches
* Statement pieces
* Modern accessories
* Simple shopping experience

---

## Main Pages

* Home
* Shop All
* New Arrivals
* Collections
* Watches
* Accessories
* About
* Contact
* Journal
* Account
* Cart
* Checkout

Use dynamic WordPress + WooCommerce data for all commerce content.

---

## Shopping Experience

Prioritize:

**Discover → Compare → Product → Purchase**

Keep navigation simple, product discovery fast, and product pages clean.

Avoid unnecessary categories and complicated navigation.

---

## Brand Direction

Relux should feel:

**Young
Clean
Confident
Modern
Accessible Premium**

Avoid:

* Old-fashioned luxury styling
* Excessive black/gold
* Heavy serif typography
* Busy layouts
* Oversized headlines
* Excessive animation

---

## Final Rule

**Less decoration. Better products. Better typography. Better experience.**
