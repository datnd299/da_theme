Brand

Name: Corvel

Category: Automatic mechanical watches
Positioning: A modern mechanical-watch label built around movement, proportion, and daily presence.
Brand idea: Precision with Presence.

Corvel designs contemporary automatic (self-winding) mechanical watches. Every timepiece runs on a mechanical movement — no quartz, no battery — and is presented through a clean, premium shopping experience for customers who want modern luxury without unnecessary excess.

Design Direction

Modern Prestige × Dark Editorial × Precision Luxury

The website should feel strong, refined, premium, architectural, contemporary, and masculine without becoming aggressive. Inspiration may come from premium watch and fashion ecommerce, but Corvel must keep an original identity.

Audience

Style-conscious customers looking for premium modern mechanical watches with strong visual presentation, clean design, and a confident luxury feel.

Collections

Corvel is organised into three permanent collections. These are defined in theme code (inc/product-categories.php) and are presented as an editorial concept across the site; they are not WooCommerce taxonomy terms.

- Foundry Collection — the core line. Robust automatic movements, brushed steel, everyday proportions.
- Frontier Collection — field and travel character. Legible dials, sapphire, higher water resistance, built for motion.
- Prestige Collection — the dress tier. Slimmer automatic calibers, polished finishing, exhibition case backs, refined detail.

Warranty

Every Corvel mechanical watch is covered by a 2-Year Limited Warranty against defects in the mechanical movement and in materials and workmanship, from the delivery date, for the original purchaser, in the United States. Full terms live on /warranty/. Normal wear, accidental or water damage beyond the rated resistance, and unauthorised service are excluded.

Ecommerce

Built for WordPress + WooCommerce.

All commerce data must remain dynamic: products, product categories, prices, sale prices, stock, product URLs, images, cart, checkout, account data. Never hard-code WooCommerce product or category data.

Homepage Goal

The homepage should feel like a luxury campaign first and an ecommerce page second.

Recommended rhythm:

Hero → Featured Products → Collections → Editorial Campaign → Product Grid → Movement / Craftsmanship → Warranty → Newsletter

Photography and products remain the visual focus.

Content Style

Copy should be short, confident, editorial, sophisticated, and easy to scan.

Example tone:

Precision with Presence.
Designed to Be Noticed.
Modern Time. Mechanical Heart.
Built Around the Movement.
Time, Made Distinct.

Avoid long paragraphs, exaggerated luxury language, generic marketing phrases, and unnecessary technical jargon. Mechanical / automatic / self-winding language is welcome where it is accurate; do not claim chronometer certification, in-house calibers, or specific accuracy figures that are not supported.

Visual Rules

Prioritize: large premium watch photography, strong negative space, editorial layouts, clean product grids, dark campaign sections balanced with light shopping sections, controlled metallic / green / warm-gold accents, subtle motion only.

Avoid: generic ecommerce templates, excessive rounded cards, heavy shadows, large gradients, oversized headlines, too many colors, excessive gold, busy backgrounds, unnecessary icons, flashy animations.

Brand Safety

Corvel must appear as an independent watch brand/store. Do not copy third-party logos, packaging, product naming, layouts, wording, imagery, or protected brand identities. Use original Corvel naming, copy, assets, and art direction.

Core Principle

Campaign visuals create desire.
Clean ecommerce design creates trust.
The mechanical watch remains the hero.
