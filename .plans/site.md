## Brand Positioning

Shopmivo is an independent general merchandise store, organized like a big-box retailer (Walmart-style), so customers can find everyday products across many categories in one place instead of shopping niche stores one at a time.

Core message:

> Everything you need, every day, in one store.

Expanded message:

> Shopmivo helps households shop tools, houseware, vehicle service essentials, gifts and toys, pet supplies, and clothing and accessories — all in one place, at everyday low prices.

The store should feel:

* Broad / one-stop-shop
* Practical
* Family-friendly
* Value-focused
* Easy to shop
* Trustworthy
* Clean and organized despite wide catalog

The store should not feel like:

* A single-niche boutique
* A luxury retailer
* A confusing catch-all dropshipping dump
* An official Walmart store or affiliate (Shopmivo is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Walmart Inc.)

## Shipping Policy Direction

Shipping Policy must follow the Crowdfused-style shipping structure used in previous projects.

Required sections:

1. Hero / Shipping Policy Overview
2. Shipping Locations
3. Order Processing & Cutoff Time
4. Estimated Delivery Time
5. Shipping Carriers
6. Shipping Costs
7. Multiple Packages
8. Tracking Your Order
9. Delivery Issues
10. Incorrect Shipping Address
11. Lost Packages
12. Damaged Packages
13. Shipping Restrictions
14. Delays
15. Customer Support CTA

Default shipping parameters:

* **Order cutoff time:** 5:00 PM (GMT-08:00) Pacific Standard Time (Los Angeles)
* **Orders after cutoff:** Begin processing on the next business day
* **Order handling time:** 1–2 business days, Monday to Friday
* **Transit time:** 5–7 business days, Monday to Friday
* **Estimated delivery time:** Usually 6–9 business days
* **Special note:** Some bulky, oversized, or partner-shipped items may take longer. Customers should be kept updated.

## Return & Refund Policy Direction

Return & Refund Policy must follow the Crowdfused-style return structure used in previous projects.

Required sections:

1. Hero
2. 30-Day Easy Returns
3. $0 Restocking Fee
4. Return Policy Overview
5. Return Costs
6. Common Return Scenarios
7. How To Return An Item
8. Return Authorization Required
9. Refund Process
10. Exchanges
11. Non-Returnable Items
12. Questions
13. Contact Information

Default return parameters:

* **Return window:** 30 days from delivery
* **Restocking fee:** $0 for eligible returns
* **Condition:** Items must be unused, undamaged, in original condition, and returned with original packaging where applicable
* **Return authorization:** Required before sending items back
* **Defective / incorrect / damaged products:** Store covers return shipping or provides a prepaid label after approval
* **Customer remorse / wrong size / wrong item / preference change:** Customer pays actual return shipping cost
* **Original shipping costs:** Non-refundable unless the return is due to store error or carrier/product damage
* **Refund timing:** Refunds are processed to the original payment method after inspection and approval, typically up to 7 days depending on bank or payment provider
* **Exchanges:** Subject to stock availability
