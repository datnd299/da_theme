
# MyTire.co — Website Brief

## Overview
MyTire is a US-based online tire retailer offering competitive prices, a wide tire selection, and great value when buying multiple tires.

## Design Direction
- Inspiration: PriorityTire.com
- Style: Modern, clean, minimal, professional e-commerce.
- Primary colors: Yellow (#FFCC00), Black (#171717), White (#FFFFFF).
- Background: Mostly white and light gray, with yellow used for buttons, promotions, and highlights.
- Typography: Inter or similar modern sans-serif.
- Layout: Spacious, structured, product-focused, and easy to navigate.
- Avoid excessive gradients, animations, decorative graphics, and oversized sections.

## Target Audience
US customers looking for affordable, reliable tires, including individual buyers and customers purchasing multiple tires.

## Core Features
- Search tires by vehicle, tire size, or brand.
- Browse tires by category and brand.
- Product listings with clear images, prices, specifications, and filters.
- Product pages with tire details, quantity selection, and add-to-cart.
- Shopping cart and checkout.
- Responsive desktop and mobile experience.

## Homepage Structure
1. Clean header with logo, navigation, search, and cart.
2. Compact hero with headline and prominent tire finder.
3. Three simple benefits: Competitive Prices, Wide Selection, Great Value on Sets.
4. Shop by tire category.
5. Featured tires in a clean product grid.
6. Small promotional banner highlighting multi-tire savings.
7. Minimal footer with customer support and policy links.

## UX Principles
- Help customers find the right tires quickly.
- Make pricing and product information easy to understand.
- Prioritize usability, speed, trust, and conversions.
- Keep the interface simple, consistent, and professional.
- Use Priority Tire as a UX reference without copying its branding or assets.

## Goal
Build a polished, trustworthy US tire e-commerce website that feels like an established online retailer, with a strong focus on competitive pricing and an effortless shopping experience.
