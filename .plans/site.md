# SITE.MD — BRICKGOSHOP

## Overview

BrickGoShop is a modern ecommerce store for:

* Building sets
* Art figures
* Designer toys
* Blind boxes
* Mini collectibles
* Display collectibles
* Accessories

Core style:

**Modern × Playful × Editorial × Collectible**

Core message:

**BUILD. COLLECT. DISPLAY.**

---

## Positioning

Create a collectible culture store, not a children's toy shop.

The experience should feel:

* Clean
* Bold
* Creative
* Curated
* Easy to shop
* Modern

Products provide most of the color; UI stays clean and neutral.

---

## Main Pages

* Home
* Shop
* Collections
* New Drops
* Culture Notes
* About
* Contact
* My Account
* Cart
* Checkout

WooCommerce product/category pages must use dynamic data.

---

## Product Categories

Main categories:

* City Buildings & Houses
* Animals, Trees & Botanicals
* City Vehicle Sets
* World War II Sets

Categories must not be hard-coded where WooCommerce data can be used dynamically.

---

## Ecommerce Rules

Use native WordPress + WooCommerce functions for:

* Products
* Categories
* Prices
* Stock
* Cart
* Checkout
* Account
* Product archives
* Search
* Sorting/filtering

The theme must work correctly after upload from local development to WooCommerce.

---

## Brand Safety

Keep demo content brand-neutral.

Do not use:

* Third-party logos
* Trademarked names
* Franchise names
* Recognizable copyrighted characters
* Unauthorized brand packaging

Use original fictional products and visuals.

---

## Final Direction

BrickGoShop should feel like a professional collectible ecommerce brand:

**CLEAN. PLAYFUL. BOLD. COLLECTIBLE.**
