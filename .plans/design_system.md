# Queen's Bracelet — Design System

## Design Goal

Build a fresh, modern, feminine bracelet boutique for charm bracelets, owl bracelets, beaded bracelets, chain bracelets, and giftable jewelry.

The site should feel:

* Fresh
* Modern
* Feminine
* Youthful
* Elegant but approachable
* Giftable
* Bracelet-focused
* Trustworthy
* GMC-safe

Avoid making the site feel like a fake luxury jewelry store, old-fashioned jewelry shop, or random accessories marketplace.

---

## Visual Style

Core style:

```txt
Fresh Modern Jewelry Boutique
```

The design should feel bright, clean, stylish, and friendly for women who like elegant but easy-to-wear bracelets.

Visual mood:

* Soft natural light
* Clean product photography
* Modern jewelry styling
* Fresh pastel accents
* Bracelet close-ups
* Wrist lifestyle photos
* Giftable jewelry scenes
* Minimal but warm ecommerce layout

Avoid:

* Heavy black/gold luxury style
* Fake designer visuals
* Dark old-fashioned jewelry look
* Overly royal/crown-heavy design
* Supplier collage images

---

## Color Palette

Use a fresher, younger color direction than traditional jewelry websites.

### Fresh Blush

```txt
#FFB7C5
```

Use for soft feminine accents, badges, and small UI highlights.

### Peach Glow

```txt
#FFD6A5
```

Use for warm background sections and giftable moments.

### Lavender Mist

```txt
#D8C7FF
```

Use for modern youthful accents and collection blocks.

### Soft Mint

```txt
#CFF5E7
```

Use for fresh supporting backgrounds and trust cards.

### Clean White

```txt
#FFFFFF
```

Use for main backgrounds, product cards, forms, and product pages.

### Champagne Gold

```txt
#D8A94E
```

Use lightly for jewelry details, icons, dividers, and premium accents.

### Deep Plum

```txt
#2F1F35
```

Use for headings, footer, strong text, and high-contrast CTA areas.

### Soft Gray

```txt
#F7F7FA
```

Use for product grid backgrounds and policy pages.

---

## Color Usage Rules

* Main background: Clean White or Soft Gray
* Hero background: Fresh Blush / Peach Glow gradient or Clean White
* Headings: Deep Plum
* Body text: muted charcoal/plum tone
* Primary CTA: Deep Plum with white text
* Secondary CTA: White with Deep Plum border
* Accent details: Champagne Gold, Fresh Blush, Lavender Mist
* Trust blocks: Soft Mint or Deep Plum

Avoid:

* Red discount-heavy UI
* Neon colors
* Too much gold
* Low contrast pastel text
* Luxury black/gold overload

---

DM Sans

Use:

* Elegant serif for main headings

* Body: simple, friendly, easy to read
* Product titles: clear and direct

## Layout Style

Use a clean modern ecommerce layout:

* Large bright hero
* Category cards with soft color accents
* Product grids with white cards
* Bracelet-focused feature sections
* Gift-focused storytelling blocks
* Trust section near bottom
* Mobile-first spacing

Recommended width:

```txt
max-w-7xl
```

Recommended spacing:

```txt
Desktop: py-20
Mobile: py-14
```

## Image Direction

Use fresh feminine jewelry photography:

* Bracelet on wrist lifestyle
* Bracelet on jewelry tray
* Owl charm close-up
* Beaded bracelet flat lay
* Gift box with bracelet
* Soft satin or linen background
* Bright daylight
* Pastel styling accents

Image rules:

* No fake designer logos
* No text inside product images
* No heavy luxury diamond styling
* No healing/medical crystal visuals
* No supplier collage images
* Bracelet should be clearly visible

---

## Product Card Style

Product cards should feel clean, young, and giftable:

* White background
* Soft pastel border or shadow
* Rounded corners 18–24px
* Clean product image
* Product title
* Price
* Material/finish note if useful
* Simple CTA

Optional badges:

```txt
Giftable
New
Best Seller
```

Avoid:

* Too many sale badges
* Fake reviews
* Countdown timers
* Luxury-style certificates
* Keyword-stuffed names

## Trust Elements

Always include:

* Secure checkout
* Tracking included
* 30-day returns
* Support email
* Shipping timeline
* Bracelet size/material details
* Jewelry care instructions

Trust section can use Deep Plum background with white text and Champagne Gold / Fresh Blush accents.

---

## Product Page Requirements

Every bracelet product page should include:

```txt
Product type
Material or finish
Bracelet length / adjustable details
Clasp type if available
Charm details
Styling use case
Gift suitability
Care instructions
Shipping note
Return eligibility
```

Avoid vague product pages with only image and price.

---

## GMC-Safe Rules

Do not use:

* Fake designer claims
* Replica / dupe wording
* Cartier / Pandora / Tiffany style references
* Unverified real gold/silver claims
* Healing crystal or medical benefit claims
* Fake reviews
* Countdown timers
* Unsupported waterproof/tarnish-free claims

Safe wording:

```txt
elegant
modern
charm bracelet
everyday style
giftable
personal expression
gold-tone
silver-tone
bracelet style
thoughtful gift
```

---

## Final Design Feeling

Queen's Bracelet should look like:

> A fresh, modern, feminine bracelet boutique offering charm bracelets, owl bracelets, beaded styles, chain bracelets, and giftable jewelry for everyday confidence.

The website must feel bright, youthful, elegant, trustworthy, bracelet-focused, and GMC-safe.
