**Positioning:**

> Patriotic Apparel & Custom Gifts For Veterans, Military Families, And Proud Americans.

**Core Message:**

**Alternative Message:**

> Wear Your Name. Carry Your Service. Share Your Pride.

# Policy Direction

Shipping policy should use the saved Crowdfused-style structure.

Default shipping parameters:

* **Order cutoff:** 5:00 PM PST
* **Handling time:** 1–2 business days, Monday to Friday
* **Transit time:** 5–7 business days, Monday to Friday
* **Estimated delivery:** Usually 6–9 business days
* **Custom note:** Personalized or made-to-order products may require additional production time before shipment.

Return policy should use the saved Crowdfused-style structure.

Default return parameters:

* **Return window:** 30 days from delivery
* **Restocking fee:** $0 for eligible returns
* **Condition:** Unused, unworn, unwashed, undamaged, and in original condition
* **Defective / incorrect / damaged:** Store covers return shipping or provides prepaid label after approval
* **Customer remorse:** Customer pays actual return shipping cost
* **Refund timing:** Typically up to 7 business days after inspection and approval
* **Custom note:** Custom or personalized products may not be eligible for return unless defective, damaged, incorrect, or required by law.
