# Patado LLC — Design System

## Design Goal

Build a warm artisan boutique website for handmade bracelets, beaded jewelry, vintage accessories, curated apparel, and artisan gifts.

The site should feel:

* Handmade
* Warm
* Personal
* Artistic
* Vintage-inspired
* Curated
* Boutique-like
* Trustworthy
* GMC-safe

Avoid making the site feel like a luxury diamond store, fake designer shop, fast-fashion store, or generic dropshipping marketplace.

---

## Visual Direction

Core style:

```txt
Elevated Etsy artisan boutique
```

The website should feel like a small creative studio with carefully selected handmade and vintage-inspired items.

Visual mood:

* Natural light
* Linen textures
* Wood surfaces
* Beads and stones
* Handmade workspace
* Jewelry trays
* Vintage fabric
* Warm neutral backgrounds
* Small-batch product styling

Avoid:

* Glossy luxury jewelry photography
* Black/gold fake luxury style
* Neon colors
* Generic marketplace product collages
* Fake designer visuals

## Typography

Recommended fonts:

```txt
Lora
Cormorant Garamond
Playfair Display
Inter
DM Sans
Source Sans 3
```

Use:

* Serif font for headings to create artisan/editorial feel
* Sans-serif font for body and product details for readability

Rules:

* Headings: warm, editorial, not too luxury
* Body: simple, readable, honest
* Product titles: clear and material-focused
* Avoid overly decorative fonts or luxury fashion typography

---

## Layout Style

Use a warm boutique ecommerce layout:

* Large lifestyle hero
* Soft category cards
* Product grids with plenty of whitespace
* Handmade story sections
* Close-up detail imagery
* Clear policy/trust blocks
* Mobile-first spacing

Recommended width:

```txt
max-w-7xl
```

Recommended spacing:

```txt
Desktop: py-20
Mobile: py-14
```

---

## Homepage Sections

Use 5 sections:

1. Hero — Handmade Jewelry & Vintage Accessories
2. Shop By Category
3. Handmade Bracelets Feature
4. Vintage Accessories & Curated Finds
5. Artisan Story / Customer Care Trust

Keep the homepage warm, visual, and personal, not overloaded with products.

## Image Direction

Use realistic artisan product photography:

* Bead bracelets on linen
* Jewelry close-ups on wood
* Hands arranging beads
* Artisan workspace
* Vintage accessories on neutral fabric
* Curated apparel with jewelry styling
* Giftable bracelet/jewelry sets

Image rules:

* No fake designer logos
* No luxury replica visuals
* No heavy stock-photo feeling
* No text inside product images
* No misleading gemstone/healing visuals
* Show material texture clearly

---

## Product Card Style

Product cards should feel handmade and curated:

* Clean White or Warm Cream background
* Soft border in Sand Beige
* Rounded corners 16–22px
* Natural product image
* Product title
* Price
* Material/handmade note if useful
* Simple CTA

Avoid:

* Too many sale badges
* Fake reviews
* Countdown timers
* Luxury-style badges
* Keyword-stuffed product titles

---

## Trust Elements

Always include:

* Secure checkout
* Tracking included
* 30-day returns
* Support email
* Shipping timeline
* Product material details
* Handmade variation note

Suggested handmade note:

```txt
Handmade pieces may include slight natural variations in color, texture, or bead pattern.
```

---

## Product Page Requirements

Every product page should include:

```txt
Product type
Materials
Size / length / fit
Handmade or curated note
Care instructions
Gift suitability if relevant
Shipping note
Return eligibility
```

For vintage-inspired items, avoid claiming “authentic vintage” unless confirmed.

---

## GMC-Safe Rules

Do not use:

* Fake designer claims
* Replica product language
* Luxury copycat visuals
* Unverified gemstone value claims
* Healing or medical crystal claims
* Fake reviews
* Countdown timers
* Misleading authentic vintage claims

Safe wording:

```txt
handmade
curated
vintage-inspired
beaded
personal style
creative details
everyday expression
small-batch feel
thoughtful gift
```

---

## Final Design Feeling

Patado LLC should look like:

> A warm artisan boutique offering handmade bracelets, beaded jewelry, vintage accessories, curated apparel, and thoughtful small gifts.

The website must feel personal, creative, trustworthy, handmade, vintage-inspired, and GMC-safe
