## Products To Avoid

For GMC safety, do not sell or promote:

* Fake Nike / Adidas / Crocs / Birkenstock / designer shoes

* 1:1 copy products
* Counterfeit branded footwear
* Unauthorized logos
* Copyrighted character footwear without authorization
* Medical orthopedic shoes with unverified claims
* Shoes claiming guaranteed pain relief or medical benefits
* Used shoes unless clearly disclosed and compliant

## GMC-Safe Rules

The site must present itself as a legitimate footwear store with clear product information.

Must include:

* Clear footwear categories
* About Us page
* Contact page
* Shipping Policy
* Return & Refund Policy
* Privacy Policy
* Terms of Service
* FAQ
* Track Order page
* Size Guide
* Product material notes
* Fit notes
* Care instructions

Avoid:

* Fake branded shoe claims
* Replica / dupe wording
* Unauthorized brand logos
* Unverified medical comfort claims
* Fake reviews
* Countdown timers
* Misleading “official” brand affiliation
* Unsupported “orthopedic” claims

## Shipping Information

Orders are processed within **2–4 business days**.

After dispatch, standard US shipping typically takes **5–10 business days** depending on destination and carrier conditions.

Tracking information is provided once an order ships.

---

## Return Policy Direction

Customers may request returns within **30 days** of delivery.

Eligible footwear should be:

* Unworn
* Undamaged
* In original condition
* Returned with original packaging where applicable

Shoes worn outdoors, damaged, heavily creased, stained, or returned without required packaging may not be eligible for return unless required by applicable law or approved by support.
