# Topgoodmart — Design System

## Design Direction

Topgoodmart is a modern American general merchandise store offering home essentials, furniture, electronics and smart lifestyle products.

Website feeling:

* Modern
* Clean
* Bright
* Trustworthy
* Organized
* Easy to shop

Avoid:

* Marketplace clutter
* Luxury aesthetics
* Dark interfaces
* Dropshipping appearance

# Product Card

Required:

* Product image
* Product title
* Brand
* Rating
* Current price
